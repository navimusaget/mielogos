What is Mielogos?

A place for an emerging creative practice to become visible to itself.

Mielogos is an international membership association for practitioners, researchers, editors, publishers, critics, organizers, and others engaged with Symbiotic Authorship.

It exists to make an emerging creative practice institutionally legible, to help people in the field find one another, and to provide durable structures through which the field can develop.

Mielogos is not a certification company, an AI vendor, a publishing house, or a claim that there is only one legitimate way to create with generative systems.

Symbiotic Authorship

Three elements. One creative relation.

A work or defined creative practice may be symbiotic when a human remains the authorial locus, the human and generative system condition the process in both directions, and that relation materially affects what the work becomes.

01

Human Authorial Locus

A human author remains responsible for the creative work placed into the world and exercises substantive authorship within the process.

02

Bidirectionally Conditioned Creative Relation

System contributions affect human decisions, and human decisions materially shape subsequent system contributions.

03

Material Causal Effect

The relation changes what the work or defined creative practice becomes in a creatively consequential rather than incidental way.

Membership

Belonging, not certification.

Membership category

Practitioner Member

For a human participant who identifies, in good faith, at least one work, project, or defined creative practice that they understand to constitute Symbiotic Authorship under the Mielogos framework.

Membership category

Associate Member

For a human participant who supports, studies, edits, publishes, teaches, researches, organizes, critiques, or otherwise engages with Symbiotic Authorship without presently making a Practitioner declaration.

Declare honestly. Belong freely. Help where you can. Respect the freedom of others. Keep responsibility for your own work.
