Membership opening soon.

The Association website and governing documents are public before applications open so prospective members can understand the institution before deciding whether to join.

What the process will ask

A concise good-faith declaration, a public name or stable pseudonym, a private administrative email, a membership category, and the minimum information needed to establish a reasonable link to the public identity under which membership is requested.

What it will not ask

No routine passport, civil-name disclosure, home address, phone number, private AI chats, complete prompt history, unpublished manuscript, detector report, or full revision archive.
