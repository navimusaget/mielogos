Privacy

Collect what is needed. Retain what remains justified.

Mielogos uses a deliberately small technical stack and a data-minimizing membership process. Private membership information is not a commercial asset.

Who is responsible

Mielogos is the International Association for Symbiotic Authorship. During the Genesis Stage, membership administration is founder-administered under the Mielogos Constitution and Membership Charter. The responsible institutional administrator is Mielogos, currently administered by Founder Navi Musaget during the Genesis Stage.

Privacy and membership contact: contact@mielogos.org.

Public website

The public site is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. Mielogos does not intentionally use advertising trackers, behavioral analytics, visitor accounts, social-tracking pixels, or a public membership database.

Ordinary hosting and network infrastructure may process standard technical information needed to deliver a web page, such as IP address, request time, requested resource, and browser or device information. Mielogos does not use that information to build visitor advertising profiles.

Membership applications

For an ordinary membership application, Mielogos collects only information reasonably needed to review, verify, communicate about, and administer membership: a public name or stable pseudonym; a private administrative email; requested membership category; a brief membership declaration; a public creative or professional reference where reasonably available; a proposed public-identity verification route or endpoint; and the required acknowledgements.

The Join form is submitted to a Google Apps Script web application. The submission is written to a private Google Sheets administrative register. Google services are also used for private Drive storage and administrative email notifications. Applicant information is not written to the public GitHub repository.

What we do not ordinarily ask for

Ordinary membership does not require a passport, government ID, home address, telephone number, date of birth, citizenship, nationality, civil name merely because a pseudonym is used, private AI chats, complete prompt histories, unpublished manuscripts, detector reports, or complete revision histories. Do not send passwords, recovery codes, private keys, or login credentials.

Why we use membership information

Mielogos uses membership information to receive and review applications; communicate with applicants and members; classify the requested membership category under the Membership Charter; establish a reasonable link to the claimed public identity; maintain the minimal membership record; administer membership rights and status changes; and protect the Association and its records against impersonation, abuse, or security incidents.

Admission, category, verification, suspension, and termination decisions are not made solely through opaque automated scoring. Administrative automation may route or record information without replacing accountable human review.

Public identity and verification

Public-identity verification is not civil-identity verification. The purpose is to establish a reasonable link between an applicant and the public identity under which membership is requested.

Mielogos records the verification result, method category, and relevant date. Temporary tokens, screenshots, and unnecessary copies of verification evidence are deleted when they are no longer needed, unless a concrete dispute or legal obligation temporarily justifies retention.

Email correspondence

If you write to contact@mielogos.org, Mielogos receives the information you choose to send together with normal email metadata. Dynadot provides the institutional domain and email routing. Incoming institutional correspondence may be forwarded to and reviewed through a Google administrative mailbox.

Retention

Unsuccessful or abandoned application materials are ordinarily deleted after 90 days unless a specific reconsideration, dispute, security, or legal need remains. Active membership records are kept while membership remains active and while specific administrative obligations remain justified.

After voluntary withdrawal, any public listing is removed promptly. Private contact information is ordinarily deleted within 90 days unless continued contact is requested or another concrete need remains. A minimal historical record may be retained where necessary for institutional accuracy.

Service providers and international processing

Mielogos currently uses GitHub Pages / GitHub for the public website; Dynadot for domain, DNS, and institutional email routing; and Google services for the membership submission endpoint (Apps Script), private Sheets / Drive storage, and administrative email.

These providers may process data in more than one country. Where data-protection law requires a transfer mechanism or safeguard, Mielogos relies on the applicable provider and legal arrangements available for that processing. The technical stack is intentionally kept small, and Mielogos does not sell private membership information to advertisers or data brokers.

Your choices and rights

Applicants and members may ask Mielogos to correct inaccurate information or withdraw an application or membership. Where applicable law grants additional rights — including access, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or complaint to a supervisory authority — those rights remain available subject to their legal conditions and exceptions.

Public Member Directory participation, if offered, is separate from admission and voluntary. No public member profile is created merely because membership is activated.

Security and changes

Mielogos limits access to non-public membership information according to operational need and uses reasonable measures appropriate to the scale and sensitivity of the information held. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure.

Material changes to how Mielogos uses membership data should be reflected in an updated notice. A privacy-notice update does not silently amend the Constitution, Membership Charter, or another governing instrument.

Effective 4 October 2026.

Data minimization is the default, not an afterthought.
