Research & Ideas

Language for a field still taking shape.

Symbiotic Authorship is not only a membership category. It raises questions about authorship, provenance, responsibility, creative relation, and the institutions that decide what counts.

Mielogos keeps those questions open to research, interpretation, and disagreement.

Foundational work

The research beneath the institutional category.

Mielogos begins from published and publicly inspectable research, but the Association does not turn a founder's scholarship into compulsory doctrine.

FOUNDATIONAL 2026 Navi Musaget

The Symbiotic Author as an Institutional Category

Authorship, Creative Provenance, and Eligibility in Human–AI Literary Production

This paper develops the work-relative category that Mielogos adopts institutionally: a human author may be a Symbiotic Author where a human authorial locus participates in a materially consequential, bidirectionally conditioned creative relation with a generative system.

Its central contribution is not a percentage test. It separates textual provenance from creative causal provenance, and classification from institutional eligibility.

Editorial principle

Label the status. Leave room for disagreement.

A research paper, an institutional interpretation, a member essay, and an exploratory note should not be made to look identical.

Mielogos will identify what a text is, who is responsible for it, and whether it represents an adopted institutional position. Publication by the Association will not automatically mean endorsement of every claim.

That distinction matters in a field where language is still being invented while institutions are already making decisions.
